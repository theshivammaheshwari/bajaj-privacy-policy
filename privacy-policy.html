Privacy Policy & Data Safety Declaration

Google Play Developer Policy Compliant

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bajaj Threads ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal and business information when you use our mobile application ("Bajaj Thread App") and related digital services.

1. Information We Collect

We strictly collect information necessary for B2B wholesale thread ordering, commercial catalog browsing, order fulfillment, and client account management:

  • Personal & Business Identification: Name, phone number, email address, shop name, trade name, GSTIN (optional), PAN (optional), and Aadhaar number (last 4 digits/voluntary for business identity verification).
  • Delivery & Logistics Data: Shipping address, city, state, pin code, and preferred transport/courier services for order dispatch.
  • Order & Transaction Records: Selected thread types, shade numbers, quantities (boxes/dozens), common order notes, order statuses, and timestamps.
  • Technical & Authentication Data: Google OAuth account identifiers (UID) and staff authentication tokens managed securely via Google Firebase.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • Processing, packing, dispatching, and tracking wholesale orders.
  • Generating official commercial order slips and invoices.
  • Communicating order status updates via WhatsApp, SMS, or phone.
  • Maintaining account security, access control, and user role management (Client, Salesman, Owner).
  • Compliance with applicable Indian commercial, tax, and auditing regulations.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

We only share data with essential infrastructure service providers necessary to operate the service:

  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud): Used for secure user authentication, Firestore encrypted database storage, and cloud infrastructure.
  • Cloudinary: Used for secure storage of authorized product catalog images.
  • Logistics & Transporters: Order shipping labels (Name, Shop, City, Phone) shared solely to fulfill physical thread delivery.

4. Device Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to connect to the cloud server, fetch real-time thread catalogs, and sync orders.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional; to send order dispatch and delivery status alerts.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Store policies and user privacy rights:

  • Account Deactivation: You can request account deactivation at any time directly within the app under the Profile → App Settings & Policies → Deactivate Account section.
  • Data Deletion Request: To request complete removal of your personal profile and account credentials, you may also email us at support@bajajthreads.com or contact us via phone.
  • Statutory Retention: Historical tax invoices and order audit logs may be retained securely as required by applicable Indian tax laws.
